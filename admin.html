Upload ticket list

Upload your Excel (.xlsx, .xls or .csv). The first sheet is used and its first row must be the column headers. It needs a Facility column and a Ticket / Report No column; every other column (Main Category, Category, Sub Category, Title, Description, etc.) is shown to technicians as-is. Uploading replaces the current list, so remove a facility or ticket by deleting its row in Excel and uploading again.
